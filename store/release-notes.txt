First release.

Survey a place in about ninety seconds: steps at the door, the width of the opening, grab bars,
turning space, noise and light. Set your own thresholds and every place is judged against them, with
the reasons and the date written out, and a drawn verdict you can read at a glance. Confirm what has
aged in one tap, and pool places with friends by sending a file. No account, no ads, and no internet
permission at all.
